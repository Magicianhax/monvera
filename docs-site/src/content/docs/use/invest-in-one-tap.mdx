One tap turns a plan into real holdings you own. When you tap invest, Monvera prices every company in the plan at its live market price, buys each one, and covers the network cost so investing is free to you. The holdings land in your own account, and you own them from that moment.

## What one tap does

<Steps>

1. You review the plan one last time, with each company's live price shown, and tap **Invest**.

2. Monvera gets a live price for every company in the plan at the same moment, so you are buying at today's real prices.

3. You approve the spend. This is the one thing you sign: it authorizes the money to leave your account, and nothing moves without it.

4. Monvera buys every company in the plan in a single step and records the details. The holdings appear in your account, and you own them.

</Steps>

## What "free to invest" means for you

Investing is free because Monvera covers the network cost of every trade, so you never fund a separate fee to make a trade happen. You pay the live market price on each company, the same spread any buyer pays, and nothing on top from Monvera. The full cost model is on [What it costs](/safety/what-it-costs/).

<Aside type="caution">
Investing spends real money on real companies, and their prices fall as well as rise. A $100 basket can be worth $80 next week. This is the point where money actually leaves your account, so read [Can I lose money?](/safety/can-i-lose-money/) first.
</Aside>

## If an invest does not go through

An invest can stop before it completes, and when it does, your money stays put rather than going through at a stale price. The common reasons are honest ones:

- **The price moved too much.** Monvera prices each company right before you invest, and that price is only good for a short window. If you wait too long, the price expires and the invest stops instead of filling at an out-of-date number. Tap invest again to get a fresh price.
- **Not enough money in your account.** If your account does not hold enough to cover the plan, the invest cannot complete. Add money first, covered in [Add money to your account](/use/add-money/), then invest again.
- **A company could not be bought.** If any company in the plan cannot be filled, the whole invest stops rather than buying you a partial basket. Nothing is spent, and you can try again.

In every one of these cases, either the whole plan goes through or none of it does. You are never left with half a basket and a spent balance.

## After you invest

Your holdings are yours, and you can sell any of them back to dollars at any time. Monvera cannot hold them back or move them without your approval. You can buy or sell one company on its own from here too, covered in [Buy or sell a single company](/use/buy-or-sell-a-single-stock/).
