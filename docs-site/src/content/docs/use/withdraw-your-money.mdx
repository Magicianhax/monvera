You can take your money out at any time, and Monvera cannot hold it back. Your holdings sit in your own account, which is yours to control, so taking money out is a two-part move: sell what you hold back to dollars, then send those dollars out to your own account. Your account is non-custodial, which means Monvera cannot move your money without your approval, as explained in [your account and recovery](/safety/your-account-and-recovery/).

## Sell your holdings back to dollars

<Steps>

1. Open a company you hold, or your portfolio screen.

2. Choose to sell, and pick how much to sell at the live price.

3. Confirm. The company is sold and your account holds dollars (USDG, the dollar-value currency your money settles in) instead.

</Steps>

Selling works the same way as [buying a single company](/use/buy-or-sell-a-single-stock/), at the live market price. You can sell one holding or all of them.

## Send your dollars out

<Steps>

1. Open the balance screen where your dollars are shown.

2. Choose to send out, and enter the destination account address you control.

3. Enter the amount and confirm. Your dollars leave Monvera and arrive in the account you named.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
Check the destination address before you confirm. A send goes to the address you enter, and Monvera cannot reverse it or pull the money back for you. Send a small amount first if you are unsure the address is right.
</Aside>

## Why Monvera cannot keep your money

Your money stays in your own account the whole time it is with Monvera, and that account is yours even if Monvera disappears. Monvera cannot move or freeze your holdings, because every sell and every send needs your approval. You can confirm this is your own non-custodial account, at an address you can look up yourself, on [your account and recovery](/safety/your-account-and-recovery/). If Autopilot is running, switching it off does not sell anything; see [set and switch off Autopilot limits](/use/set-and-revoke-autopilot-limits/).
