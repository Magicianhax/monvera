You make a plan by telling Vera a goal in plain words and an amount, and she returns a named basket of real companies, each with a one-line reason and an honest read on what could go wrong. Nothing is bought yet. A plan is something you read first and invest only when you are ready.

## Tell Vera a goal

<Steps>

1. Open Monvera and sign in. Your account is created for you the first time you sign in, so there is nothing to install and nothing to write down.

2. Tap **Tell Vera a goal** and type what you want in plain words, like "grow my money steadily for five years" or "something safer than stocks alone."

3. Enter an amount. You can start from one dollar, so you can make a real plan with a small amount while you learn.

4. Vera returns a named basket. Each row is a real company or fund, with a live price, a chart, a one-line reason it is there, and a plain read on the risk.

</Steps>

## What Vera hands back

Vera hands back a named basket of real holdings you can read line by line. Here is the example this documentation uses throughout. The plan is named **Steady Growth**, and it holds four things:

- **Apple (AAPL)**, a large, steady company, with the plain read that even big companies fall.
- **Nvidia (NVDA)**, faster growth and larger swings, so it can drop hard in a bad month.
- **The S&P 500**, a fund holding 500 large US companies, so no single company sinks you.
- **US Treasuries**, short-term government debt, the calm part of the basket that barely moves.

You can read the whole basket before you commit a cent. The next page, [Read your plan](/use/read-your-plan/), walks through how to judge each row.

<Aside type="note">
A plan is not a recommendation. Vera gives you options with reasons and a risk read, not advice. See [Is this investment advice?](/safety/is-this-investment-advice/).
</Aside>

## Before you invest

You do not have to invest the plan Vera returns, and you can change it first. You can drop any company or change any amount before anything is bought, covered in [Tweak a plan before you invest](/use/tweak-a-plan-before-you-invest/). Nothing is committed until you tap invest.

You can also see the whole flow with no money and no account on the demo, which is the calmest way to make your first plan. Open [Try the demo](/start/try-the-demo/) first if you want to look before you spend.
