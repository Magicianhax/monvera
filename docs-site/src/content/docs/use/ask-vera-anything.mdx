The chat is not just for building plans anymore. Vera understands what you are asking for and does the right thing with it: answers a question from real data, opens the right panel, prefills a ticket, or builds a plan. She never invents a number, and she never moves money on her own — every action still ends with you confirming it.

## What you can say

- **"What do I hold?" / "How am I doing?"** — she answers from your live portfolio: positions, values, today's moves, and your cash.
- **"Review my portfolio"** — runs the full [portfolio review](/use/review-your-portfolio/): concentration, theme overlap, and your mix's own year against the S&P 500.
- **"Rebalance me" / "make it less tech-heavy"** — she works out which of your names sit furthest under target and proposes a plan that tops them up with your cash. Buys only; selling stays your manual call.
- **"How's Nvidia doing?" / "What moved today?"** — answered from live day moves across the whole universe. If the data is not there, she says so instead of guessing.
- **"Buy $20 of Tesla" / "Sell some $MONVERA"** — she opens the right ticket for you, set to the right side. You enter the amount and confirm; the signature is always yours.
- **"Tell me when NVDA drops below $150"** — sets a real [price alert](/use/set-a-price-alert/) on your account and confirms what she is watching.
- **"Set up Autopilot"** — she asks for the amount, cadence, and how bold to be (with tappable quick answers), then opens [Autopilot](/use/autopilot/) with your numbers already filled in. You review and hit Start.
- **"What's your track record?"** — the real on-chain numbers: plans committed, invests executed, dollars placed, with links to the transactions.
- **"What is Monvera?" / "What does it cost?" / "Is my money safe?"** — answered from the same facts as these docs, in the same voice.

<Steps>

1. Open the chat (Vera's orb) and type in your own words. No commands, no tickers required.

2. If Vera asks you something back, tap one of the quick answers under her message — it lands in the composer where you can edit it before sending.

3. Whatever she proposes — a plan, a ticket, a panel — nothing happens until you read it and confirm it yourself.

</Steps>

## What she will never do

- **Invent a figure.** Her answers cite the live numbers she was given. Missing data gets an honest "I don't have that."
- **Act without you.** Opening a ticket or a panel is a handoff. Plans need your tap to invest. Autopilot needs your one-time authorization.
- **Publish your conversation.** Chat history is stored privately with your account. What is public is her track record: the plans and invests she signs on-chain.

<Aside type="note" title="Private conversation, public actions">
  Your messages are yours. What Vera commits on-chain — recorded before any money moves — is the plan itself, so anyone can check her track record without ever seeing your chat.
</Aside>
