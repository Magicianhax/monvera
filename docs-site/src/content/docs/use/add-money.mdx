## What USDG is

USDG is the dollar-value currency your money settles in, and one USDG tracks one US dollar. When you add money, you are adding USDG. When you invest, your USDG buys real companies. When you sell, you get USDG back. You never have to think about it beyond that: it is the dollar balance your account spends and receives.

## How to add money

<Steps>

1. Sign in and open your account. Your balance is shown in USDG, and it starts at zero until you add money.

2. Tap **Add money**. Monvera shows you where to send USDG so it lands in your Monvera account.

3. Send USDG to that destination from wherever you hold it. Send only USDG, and send it to the destination Monvera shows you, not anywhere else.

4. Wait for the balance to update. Once the transfer settles, your USDG balance goes up and you can invest.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
This is real money. Send only USDG, and send it only to the destination Monvera shows you inside your account. Money sent to the wrong place cannot be reversed by Monvera. If anyone messages you a different address to send to, it is not Monvera. Check [Staying safe from scams](/safety/staying-safe-from-scams/) before you send.
</Aside>

## How much you need

You need at least one dollar of USDG to invest, because the minimum is one dollar. Add a little more than the amount you plan to invest so the live market price on each trade is comfortably covered. If your account is short when you tap invest, the invest stops and nothing is spent, covered in [Invest a plan in one tap](/use/invest-in-one-tap/).

## Taking money back out

Your money is yours, and you can take it out at any time by selling your holdings back to USDG and withdrawing to your own account. Monvera cannot hold your money back. The full path is on [Your account and recovery](/safety/your-account-and-recovery/).
