Tell Monvera a price and it notifies you when a company reaches it, so you do not have to watch the chart yourself. An alert only tells you; it never buys or sells anything on its own.

## Set an alert

<Steps>

1. Open the company from the [explore screen](/use/explore-whats-available/), the [screener](/use/use-the-screener/), or your [watchlist](/use/build-a-watchlist/).

2. Tap the alert bell on the company's page.

3. Enter the price you want to hear about. You can be told when the price rises to that level or falls to it.

4. Save the alert. Monvera notifies you the next time the company's live price reaches it.

</Steps>

## See and remove your alerts

Your alerts live on the alerts screen, each showing the company and the price you set. Remove any alert there when you no longer want it. Alerts are tied to your sign-in, so they stay with your account across devices.

<Aside type="caution">
You can keep up to 20 active alerts at once. If you try to add a 21st, Monvera asks you to remove one first. An alert is only a heads-up: reaching your price does not buy the company for you. When you want to act, [buy it at its live price](/use/buy-or-sell-a-single-stock/) or ask Vera to [build a plan](/use/make-your-first-plan/).
</Aside>

## Alerts versus Autopilot

An alert tells you and waits for you to act. If you would rather Monvera invest for you on a schedule without checking in each time, that is [Autopilot](/use/autopilot/), which invests a set amount on a schedule inside limits you set.
