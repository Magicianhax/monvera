Point your camera at any product — a soda can, a pair of sneakers, a laptop — and Vera identifies it, then maps it to the **listed companies actually behind it**: the maker, the parent company, the suppliers whose parts are inside, the retailers that sell it. Each connection comes with one honest sentence explaining why it's there. Pick an amount, and the plan runs through the exact same pipeline as every other Monvera invest: a signed risk assessment, verified and recorded on-chain, then real buys at live quotes.

<Aside type="note" title="A holder feature">
Scan to Buy unlocks when your wallet holds **100,000 $MONVERA** (about $100 at launch). The Scan card on the home screen shows your progress, and the [token screen](/start/the-monvera-token/) is where you buy — gasless, in the same wallet that holds your stocks. Everything else in Monvera stays free; this is the premium layer, not a toll.
</Aside>

## How it works

<Steps>

1. **Scan.** Tap the Scan card on the home screen, then take a photo or upload one from your gallery.

2. **Vera looks.** She identifies the product and brand, and maps them against the 95 listed stocks and funds — only through real, explainable relationships: maker, parent, supplier, component, retailer, or direct competitor.

3. **Read the connections.** Each company card shows the relationship and one plain sentence of reasoning, plus a weight for how central it is to the product. No force-fits: the mapping is validated against the real asset list, and anything Vera can't honestly justify doesn't appear.

4. **Pick an amount and build.** The minimum is about $11 per company (the venue's floor). From there it's the plan you already know — review it, tweak it, and invest in one tap.

</Steps>

## When there's nothing to buy

Honesty is the feature. If the brand behind your photo isn't among the listed companies — and there's no genuine supplier or competitor link either — Vera says exactly that: what she recognized, and that there's nothing honest to buy. She'll offer to build a regular plan instead. She will never stretch a connection to make a sale.

<Aside type="tip" title="It counts toward Vera's record">
A Scan to Buy plan is a real Vera plan: she signs the risk assessment, the verifier contract checks it on-chain before any money moves, and the whole thing lands in her public, re-checkable track record — same as every other invest.
</Aside>
