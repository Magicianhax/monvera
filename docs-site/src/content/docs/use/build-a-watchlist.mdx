Save any company to a watchlist and its live price and chart are one tap away, without hunting for it each time. A watchlist is your own shortlist of companies you want to keep an eye on before you decide anything.

<figure class="shot"><img src="/screens/market-list.webp" alt="The market list with a star on each company to add it to a watchlist" class="phone-shot" /><figcaption>Tap the star on any company to add it to your watchlist.</figcaption></figure>


## Add a company to your watchlist

<Steps>

1. Open a company from the [explore screen](/use/explore-whats-available/), the [screener](/use/use-the-screener/), or a plan Vera built you.

2. Tap the star on the company's page.

3. The company moves to your watchlist. Tap the star again any time to take it off.

</Steps>

Your watchlist is tied to your sign-in, so it follows you when you sign back in on another device.

## See your watchlist

Your watchlist lives on the watchlist screen. Each row shows the real company name and logo, its live price, and today's move, so you can scan your shortlist at a glance. Tap any row to open the full chart.

## A watchlist is a shortlist, not a plan

A watchlist does not buy anything or commit any money. It only saves companies you want to watch. When you decide to act, you can [buy a single company](/use/buy-or-sell-a-single-stock/) at its live price, or ask Vera to [build you a plan](/use/make-your-first-plan/).

<Aside type="tip">
Want to be told the moment a company reaches a price you have in mind? [Set a price alert](/use/set-a-price-alert/) instead of checking the chart yourself.
</Aside>
