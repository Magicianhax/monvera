You can change any amount or drop any company in a plan before you invest, and nothing is committed until you tap invest. A plan Vera hands back is a starting point, not a locked decision. If a company worries you or the split is not what you want, change it, and the plan is yours.

## What you can change

You can reshape a plan in three ways before anything is bought:

- **Change how much goes into a company.** Move the amount up or down on any row to give a company more or less of your money.
- **Drop a company.** Remove any row you do not want, and the plan holds the rest.
- **Change the total amount.** Invest more or less than Vera's starting amount, from one dollar up.

## Reshape the Steady Growth example

<Steps>

1. Open the plan. The **Steady Growth** example holds Apple, Nvidia, the S&P 500, and US Treasuries, each with its own amount.

2. Say Nvidia's risk read worries you. Lower its amount, or tap to drop Nvidia entirely, and the plan holds the other three.

3. Move the freed-up amount into US Treasuries, the calm part of the basket, if you want a steadier plan. The split updates as you go.

4. Check the new plan reads the way you want. The reasons and risk reads stay on every row you keep, covered in [Read your plan](/use/read-your-plan/).

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
Dropping the spread-out holdings and putting everything into one company removes the protection a basket gives you. A plan of one company falls as hard as that company does. See [Read your plan](/use/read-your-plan/) for why the spread matters, and [Can I lose money?](/safety/can-i-lose-money/) before you commit.
</Aside>

## Nothing is committed until you invest

No money moves while you tweak a plan. You can change amounts, drop companies, and change your mind as many times as you want, and nothing is bought until you tap invest. Up to that moment, no money has left your account. When the plan reads the way you want, move on to [Invest a plan in one tap](/use/invest-in-one-tap/).
