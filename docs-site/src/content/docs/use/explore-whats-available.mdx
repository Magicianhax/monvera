You can browse all 95 real companies and funds Monvera offers, sorted by theme and by what moved today, each with a live price and a chart. There are 86 stocks and 9 funds, and you can look at every one before you buy or ask Vera for a plan. Browsing costs nothing and does not commit you to anything.

## Browse by theme

Companies and funds are grouped by theme, so you can look at a whole area at once instead of scrolling one long list. You can open a company like Apple or Nvidia directly, or start from a theme and see what sits inside it.

The 9 funds each cover a whole area in one holding, and they are shown by what they track:

- **The S&P 500**, 500 large US companies in one fund.
- **The Nasdaq 100**, the largest companies on the Nasdaq.
- **US Treasuries**, short-term US government debt, the calm end of the range.
- **Silver**, the metal.
- **Semiconductors**, the chip industry.
- **S&P 500 Momentum**, the large US companies that have been rising.
- **The Tech Sector**, US technology companies.
- **Oil**, the oil market.
- **South Korea**, large South Korean companies.

A fund spreads your money across many companies at once, which is why Vera often puts one in a basket. See [Read your plan](/use/read-your-plan/) for how a fund like the S&P 500 steadies a plan.

## See what moved today

The daily movers show which companies and funds rose or fell the most today, with the live price change on each. It is a quick way to see where the day's action is before you decide anything. A big move up is not a reason to buy, and a big move down is not a reason to sell. Prices swing both ways, covered in [Can I lose money?](/safety/can-i-lose-money/).

## Check any price and chart

Every company and fund has a live price and a chart you can open, so you can see how it has moved over time before you commit. The price you see is the live market price, the same one any buyer pays. When a company has no live price feed, Monvera shows that honestly rather than displaying a made-up number. Where those prices come from is on [Where prices come from](/how/where-prices-come-from/).
