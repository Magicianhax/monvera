You can buy or sell one company on its own, outside any plan, at its live price. You do not have to use Vera or make a basket to own a single company. Find Apple, tap buy, choose an amount, and you own it. Selling works the same way in reverse.

## Buy one company

<Steps>

1. Find the company you want. Search by name, like Apple, or browse the full list on [Explore what's available](/use/explore-whats-available/).

2. Open its page. You see its live price and a chart, so you know what you are paying before you commit.

3. Tap **Buy** and enter an amount in dollars. You can start from one dollar.

4. Approve the spend. This is the one thing you sign, and it authorizes the money to leave your account. Monvera covers the network cost, so buying is free to you.

5. You own it. The holding appears in your account at the price it filled.

</Steps>

## Sell one company

<Steps>

1. Open the holding in your account.

2. Tap **Sell** and choose how much to sell, part or all of it.

3. Approve the sale. Your holding turns back into USDG, the dollar-value currency your account settles in.

4. The dollars land in your balance. You can invest them again or take them out.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
Buying a single company puts all of that money into one company, with none of the spread a basket gives you. One company can fall hard on a bad day, more than a mixed basket would. Read [Can I lose money?](/safety/can-i-lose-money/) before you commit, and see [Read your plan](/use/read-your-plan/) for why a basket spreads the risk.
</Aside>

## If a buy or sell does not go through

A buy or sell can stop before it completes, and when it does, nothing is spent. The reasons are the same honest ones as investing a plan:

- **The price moved too much.** Monvera prices the company right before you trade, and that price is only good for a short window. If you wait too long, it expires and the trade stops instead of filling at an out-of-date price. Try again to get a fresh price.
- **Not enough money.** A buy needs enough USDG in your account to cover it. Add money first, covered in [Add money to your account](/use/add-money/), then try again.
- **The company could not be traded right then.** If the trade cannot be filled, it stops rather than filling badly. Nothing is spent, and you can try again.
