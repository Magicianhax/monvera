You need a phone and a few dollars. There is nothing to install, no seed phrase to write down, and no network fee to fund. You sign in with an email or a social login, and your account is created for you.

## The short list

<Steps>

1. **A phone or a computer with a browser.** Monvera runs in the browser at [monvera.best](https://monvera.best). There is no app to download.

2. **An email address or a social login.** You sign in with that, and Monvera creates your account for you on the spot. You do not set up anything separate, and you never write down a secret recovery phrase.

3. **A few dollars.** You can invest from $1. Your money is held as USDG, a dollar-value currency that every trade settles in, so a dollar in USDG stays worth about a dollar. See [Add money](/use/add-money/) for the one supported way to get money in.

</Steps>

## What you do not need

You do not need to fund a network fee, because Monvera covers the cost of every trade. Investing is free to you in that sense: there is no platform fee and no per-trade network charge on your side. See [What it costs](/safety/what-it-costs/).

You do not need to install a browser extension, manage a separate crypto app, or copy a long address to get started. Your account is created from your sign-in, and it is yours.

<Aside type="note">
Monvera never asks for or shows a private key, and it never asks you to write down a recovery phrase. If anything ever asks you for one in Monvera's name, it is not Monvera. See [Staying safe from scams](/safety/staying-safe-from-scams/).
</Aside>

## Before you sign in

Check that Monvera is available where you are. It is not available in the US, Canada, the UK, or Switzerland, and the account and investing parts of the site are blocked from those countries. See [Where Monvera is available](/start/where-its-available/).
