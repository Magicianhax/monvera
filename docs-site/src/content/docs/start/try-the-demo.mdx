Open [monvera.best/demo](https://monvera.best/demo) to see the whole flow with no money and no account. You type a goal, Vera builds a basket of real companies, and you walk through the invest screen, all without signing in or spending anything.

The demo is the best place to start if you have never bought a stock and want to see what happens before you commit. Nothing in it moves real money.

## Walk through it in five steps

<Steps>

1. **Open the demo.** Go to [monvera.best/demo](https://monvera.best/demo) in your browser. There is no sign-in and no account.

2. **Tell Vera a goal.** Type something in plain words, like "grow my money steadily for five years," and an amount. This is the same box you would use for real.

3. **Read the basket she builds.** Vera returns a named plan, for example **Steady Growth** over Apple (AAPL), Nvidia (NVDA), the S&P 500, and US Treasuries. Each row is a real company or fund with a reason it is there and a plain read on what could go wrong.

4. **Look at the invest screen.** You see exactly what one tap would buy and what each part is priced at. In the demo, tapping through does not spend anything.

5. **Come back when you want the real thing.** When you are ready, go to [monvera.best](https://monvera.best), sign in, and make a real plan. See [Make your first plan](/use/make-your-first-plan/).

</Steps>

## What the demo does and does not do

The demo shows the real flow with real companies and live prices, so what you see matches the actual product. It does not create an account, take any money, or place any trade. Nothing you do in the demo is saved to a real account, and no real holdings are bought.

<Aside type="caution">
Because the demo takes no money and creates no account, it also does not save your work. When you want to invest for real, you start fresh at [monvera.best](https://monvera.best) with a signed-in account. See [What you need to start](/start/what-you-need/).
</Aside>

## Before you switch to the real thing

Check that Monvera is available where you are. It is not available in the US, Canada, the UK, or Switzerland. See [Where Monvera is available](/start/where-its-available/).
