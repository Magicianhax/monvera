Monvera is an AI broker for real tokenized stocks and funds. You tell Vera a goal and an amount, she builds a named basket of real companies each with a reason and a plain risk read, and one tap invests it. You own the result from $1, and you can check every part of it.

## What Vera does for you

Vera turns a goal in plain words into a named basket of real companies. You type something like "grow my money steadily for five years" and an amount, and she returns a basket with each company chosen for a reason and a one-line read on what could go wrong.

Here is the example this documentation uses throughout. The plan is named **Steady Growth**, and it holds four things:

- **Apple (AAPL)**, a large, steady company, with the plain read that even big companies fall.
- **Nvidia (NVDA)**, faster growth and larger swings, so it can drop hard in a bad month.
- **The S&P 500**, a fund holding 500 large US companies, so no single company sinks you.
- **US Treasuries**, short-term government debt, the calm part of the basket that barely moves.

Each row is a real company or fund with a live price and a chart. You can read the whole basket before you commit a cent.

<Aside type="note">
A basket is not a recommendation. Vera gives you options with reasons and a risk read, not advice. See [Is this investment advice?](/safety/is-this-investment-advice/).
</Aside>

## What you actually own

You own real holdings in a Monvera account that belongs to you. When you invest, your money buys real tokenized stocks and funds, and they sit in your own account. Monvera cannot move them without your approval, and you can sell back to dollars at any time. More on this in [Your account and recovery](/safety/your-account-and-recovery/).

There are 95 companies and funds to choose from: 86 stocks and 9 funds. You can browse them all, buy or sell one on its own, or let Vera build a basket for you.

## Why the AI is one you can check

Vera is a registered on-chain agent, and the risk read behind every plan is signed by her and recorded permanently when your trade happens. Vera signs the risk assessment, which proves she authored it. You separately approve the spend, which authorizes the money. The two are different actions with different meanings, and the record of hers cannot be edited afterward.

This is the part that makes Monvera different from a chatbot that gives stock tips and forgets them. Her track record is kept honest because it is written down where anyone can read it. See [Accountable AI](/how/accountable-ai/) for how that works and what it does and does not prove.

## What it costs and what you risk

Monvera charges no platform fee, no account fee, and no subscription, and it covers the network cost of every trade. You pay the live market price on each trade, the same spread any buyer pays. Full detail on [What it costs](/safety/what-it-costs/).

You can lose money. These are real companies, and real prices fall, so a $100 basket can be worth $80 next week. Monvera is not a bank, is not insured, and promises no return. Read [Can I lose money?](/safety/can-i-lose-money/) before you invest.
