Monvera is not available in the US, Canada, the UK, or Switzerland. If your connection comes from one of these, the site returns a restricted page and you cannot open an account or invest. This is the canonical statement of Monvera's limits, and every other page links here instead of repeating it.

## The three things to know before you start

**Monvera is not available in the US, Canada, the UK, or Switzerland. Nothing here is investment advice. Backtests are history, not promises.**

Read these once. They apply everywhere on Monvera, and the rest of this page explains each one.

## Why you might see a restricted page

Monvera checks the country your connection comes from and blocks four of them. Access is decided by the country attached to your internet connection, not by your account, because the check happens before any account exists. If you connect from the US, Canada, the UK, or Switzerland, the site returns an HTTP 451 response and shows the restricted page instead of the app.

<Aside type="caution">
If you reach the restricted page, that is the answer. Monvera does not have a workaround, an allowlist, or a way to appeal it from support. Using a tool to disguise where you connect from does not make Monvera available to you and may break other terms.
</Aside>

The public parts of Monvera, like browsing companies and reading prices, are open more widely. The blocked response applies to the account and investing parts of the site for the four listed countries.

## Nothing here is investment advice

Vera gives you options, not advice. Her baskets come with a reason for each company and a plain read on what could go wrong, and that is where her job ends. She does not tell you what you should do with your money, and nothing on Monvera or in this documentation is investment advice. See [Is this investment advice?](/safety/is-this-investment-advice/).

## Backtests are history, not promises

A backtest shows what a rule did on past prices, and that is all it shows. Monvera publishes rule-based strategies, and each one carries a backtest run over real past prices so you can see how the rule behaved. Past behavior is not a forecast, the future can differ, and you can lose money on a rule that looked good on history. See [Backtests are history, not promises](/safety/backtests-are-history-not-promises/).

## If something looks wrong

Contact Monvera on X at [@monvera_best](https://x.com/monvera_best), the same channel the restricted page points to. Monvera never sends you a direct message first and never asks for a private key or a password. See [Staying safe from scams](/safety/staying-safe-from-scams/) and [Get help](/safety/get-help/).
