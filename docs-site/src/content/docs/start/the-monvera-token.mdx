## Buy & sell in the app

The app now trades $MONVERA directly. On the home screen you'll see a live price pill (tap it for the full token screen: chart, stats, and the buy/sell panel).

- **Buying is gasless.** You pay in USDG from your Monvera wallet — the same sponsored, no-ETH-needed flow as every stock buy. Orders are routed for the best available price across the chain's pools (via LiFi, with a direct Uniswap fallback), with slippage capped at 3%.
- **Selling is gasless too, with one wrinkle on your very first sell.** The token contract doesn't support signature-based approvals, so your wallet needs to sign one tiny on-chain approval once. The app covers it: you'll see "Preparing your wallet…" for a few seconds while we send your wallet a sliver of ETH to pay for that single approval. Every sell after that is instant and fully sponsored.
- **Same wallet as your stocks.** $MONVERA sits in your normal Monvera wallet next to your tokenized stocks — self-custody, visible on Blockscout, withdrawable any time.

### The holder gate

The token screen shows your progress toward **100,000 $MONVERA** (about $100 at launch). Holding that much marks you as a holder — the tier that unlocks holder features as they ship, starting with **Scan to Buy** (photograph any product and Vera maps it to listed companies and invests, recorded on-chain). Per [our roadmap](https://monvera.best/roadmap) standard: it's *unlocking soon*, and we say so in the app rather than pretending it's live.

## What the token is for

Vera is a working agent: she routes real volume through a live venue, records a signed risk assessment for every plan, and sells portfolio-plan building to other agents on Virtuals ACP. $MONVERA is the community's way to back that agent inside the Virtuals agent economy.

What that means today: a stake in the agent, on the same chain as the product, with fixed supply and locked tokenomics. Where it can go — paying for Vera's agent-to-agent work, early access to new capabilities, community input on strategies — follows the same standard as [our roadmap](https://monvera.best/roadmap): we say *exploring* until it ships.
