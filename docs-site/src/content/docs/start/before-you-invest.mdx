Yes, you can lose money. These are real companies at real prices, so a $100 basket can be worth $80 next week. Monvera is not a bank, it is not insured, and it promises no return. Read this page once before you put money in.

## Where Monvera does not work

| Country | Status |
| --- | --- |
| United States | Blocked |
| Canada | Blocked |
| United Kingdom | Blocked |
| Switzerland | Blocked |
| Everywhere else | Available |

This is enforced, not a formality. Monvera checks the country your connection comes from before any account exists. From one of the four, the site returns an HTTP 451 and shows a restricted page instead of the app.

<Aside type="caution">
There is no allowlist, no appeal, and no workaround through support. Using a tool to disguise where you connect from does not make Monvera available to you, and it may break other terms you have agreed to.
</Aside>

## What Vera is not

**Not advice.** Vera gives you options with a reason for each name and a plain read on what could go wrong. That is where her job ends. She does not tell you what you should do with your money, and nothing in these docs is investment advice. [More](/safety/is-this-investment-advice/).

**Not a forecast.** Monvera's strategies each carry a backtest run over real past prices. A backtest shows what a rule did, not what it will do. A rule that looked good on history can still lose you money. [More](/safety/backtests-are-history-not-promises/).

**Not insured.** No deposit protection, no guarantee, no floor under a bad month.

## What it costs

Monvera charges you nothing — no platform fee, no account fee, no network fee. The one real cost is the trading venue's spread, already inside every quote you see. Two minimums shape what you can build: you can start from $1, but each name in a plan needs about $11. [What it costs](/safety/what-it-costs/) has both numbers in full.

## What stays yours

Your holdings sit in a wallet you own. Monvera cannot move them without your approval, and you can sell back to dollars at any time. The flip side is that recovery is on you — read [Your account and recovery](/safety/your-account-and-recovery/) before you fund it, not after.

If something looks wrong, Monvera is on X at [@monvera_best](https://x.com/monvera_best). Monvera never messages you first and never asks for a private key or a password.
