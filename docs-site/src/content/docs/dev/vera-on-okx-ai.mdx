## How paying works

Every paid endpoint speaks x402: an unpaid call returns HTTP 402 with a payment challenge, your wallet signs a gasless USDT authorization, the OKX broker settles it on X Layer, and the same call retried with the payment header returns the result. From OKX.AI or any onchainos-enabled agent this is automatic — you just see a price confirmation.

Payments settle in **USDT0 or USDG on X Layer** (chain 196). Trade execution is separate: the legs Vera returns are swaps on **Solana**, where xStocks liquidity actually lives, executed by your own wallet through the OKX DEX aggregator. Buys and sells both work, from any amount.

## Her track record is on-chain

Every paid plan or basket is committed to **VeraRecordV2** on X Layer at
[`0xd97cd1a25484252bf234ab384c3818b05e6594e0`](https://www.oklink.com/x-layer/address/0xd97cd1a25484252bf234ab384c3818b05e6594e0).
Each `RecommendationCommitted` event binds the plan hash, the payer, the amount paid, the risk score and the leg count under one EIP-712 signature from her agent key — so a third party cannot forge entries, and anyone can audit what Vera recommended, to whom, and when. Backtests are history, not promises; the record makes the history checkable.
