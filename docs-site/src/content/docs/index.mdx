Monvera is an AI broker for real tokenized stocks and funds on Robinhood Chain. You tell Vera a goal and an amount, she builds a named basket of real companies (Apple, Nvidia, the S&P 500, US Treasuries), each with a one-line reason and a plain read on the risk, and one tap invests it. You own the result, and you can check every part of it.

Not available in the US, Canada, the UK, or Switzerland. Nothing here is investment advice. Backtests are history, not promises.
