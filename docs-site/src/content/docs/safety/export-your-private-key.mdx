Monvera is self-custody: the account is yours, held by a key that only you control, and Monvera can never move your money. Because it is genuinely yours, you can export the private key and carry the account into another wallet like MetaMask. This is also the single most dangerous thing you can do in the app, so it sits behind a warning and a checkbox you have to tick on purpose.

## What a private key is

Your private key is the one password to your whole account. It is not a login you can reset. Anyone who has it can take everything in the account, instantly and permanently, and no one can undo that or get the money back. Think of it as the key to a safe: useful in your hands, ruinous in anyone else's.

## Before you export, understand this

<Aside type="danger" title="These cannot be undone">
  - **Anyone who sees the key can take everything.** A screenshot, a screen-share, a phishing site, a person looking over your shoulder. That is all it takes.
  - **It cannot be recovered.** If you lose the key, no one, including Monvera, can restore it or the money behind it.
  - **Monvera never stores it and can never reset it.** There is no support line for a stolen or lost key. Nobody can help after the fact.
  - **No real staff will ever ask for it.** Anyone who does, in a chat, a DM, an email, or a form, is trying to rob you. See [Staying safe from scams](/safety/staying-safe-from-scams/).
</Aside>

## How to export

<figure class="shot">
  <img src="/screens/export-key-settings.webp" alt="The Export private key row under Security in Settings" class="phone-shot" />
  <figcaption>You will find it in Settings, under Security.</figcaption>
</figure>

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings** and, under **Security**, tap **Export private key**.

2. Read the warning in full. Tick the box to confirm you understand that anyone with the key controls your money, that it cannot be recovered, and that no one from Monvera will ever ask you for it.

3. Tap **I understand, reveal my key**. A secure window from your wallet provider opens and shows the key. Monvera's own code, servers, and logs never see it.

4. Move it somewhere safe offline, or import it straight into your other wallet. Never paste it into a website, a message, or a shared screen.

</Steps>

## When you would actually do this

You do not need to export anything to use Monvera. The key lives safely inside your account and signs your trades for you. Exporting is for one thing: taking the same account into another wallet you control. If that is not what you are doing, there is no reason to reveal the key, and every reason not to.

<Aside type="note">
  You do not have to write anything down to keep using Monvera. Your account is created and secured for you when you sign in. Export the key only if you specifically want to use the account elsewhere.
</Aside>
