The only real Monvera addresses are monvera.best and docs.monvera.best. Monvera never messages you first, never asks for a private key, and never asks you to move your money somewhere to "release" or "verify" it. Anything that does one of those is not Monvera.

## The only real addresses

Two web addresses are Monvera, and nothing else is. The app lives at [monvera.best](https://monvera.best) and these docs live at docs.monvera.best. Any other spelling, any lookalike with an extra word or a different ending, and any link sent to you in a message is not Monvera. Type the address yourself rather than following a link from a stranger.

## What Monvera will never do

Monvera never contacts you first and never asks for a secret.

- Monvera never sends you a direct message on any app or social network. The only place Monvera posts is X at [@monvera_best](https://x.com/monvera_best), and the only ways to reach support are that account and [support@monvera.best](mailto:support@monvera.best). Monvera will not slide into your messages asking you to act.
- Monvera never asks for or shows a private key, a seed phrase, or a password. See [your account and recovery](/safety/your-account-and-recovery/) for why you never need one.
- Monvera never asks you to send money to a new account to claim a reward, fix a problem, or pass a check.

<Aside type="caution" title="Nobody real needs your key or your urgency">
Real support never needs your private key and never rushes you. If a message creates pressure to act now or share a secret, stop and check it against [get help](/safety/get-help/) before doing anything.
</Aside>

## What is actually enforced

Some of Monvera's honesty is enforced on-chain, and some things you should not assume.

Enforced on-chain: Vera signs the risk assessment behind each plan with her own on-chain key, and that assessment is checked and written into the same transaction that buys your stocks, so her record cannot be edited afterward. You separately approve the spend, which is the only step that moves your money. Anyone can confirm this on the public block explorer. See [accountable AI](/how/accountable-ai/) for the plain version and [verify Vera](/dev/verify-vera/) for the step-by-step check.

Not assured: Monvera has not been externally audited. There is no third-party security audit of the code, no insurance on your money, and no deposit protection. Do not read Monvera's on-chain records as an audit or a guarantee. They prove who did what and when. They do not prove the code is free of bugs.

Being able to check the record does not stop you losing money to the market. See [can I lose money](/safety/can-i-lose-money/). If something looks wrong, see [get help](/safety/get-help/).
