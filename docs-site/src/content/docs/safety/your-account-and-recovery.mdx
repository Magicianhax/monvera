Your money stays in your own account, and if you lose your phone you get it back by signing in again the same way you first signed in. Your account is non-custodial, which means Monvera cannot move your money without your approval, and it is a smart account tied to your sign-in, not to one device.

## Whose money it is

Your money is yours, not Monvera's. When you invest, the holdings in the "Steady Growth" plan over Apple (AAPL), Nvidia (NVDA), the S&P 500 (SPY), and US Treasuries (SGOV) sit in an account that only you can spend from. Monvera builds the plan and shows you the account, but it cannot take the money out. Every spend is approved by you.

Because the account is non-custodial, it also keeps working even if Monvera does not. Your holdings live on Robinhood Chain at an account address you can look up on the public block explorer, so they do not depend on Monvera staying online. You can [take your money out](/use/withdraw-your-money/) at any time.

## If you lose your phone

You recover the same account by signing in again with the same method you used the first time.

<Steps>

1. Open [monvera.best](https://monvera.best) on any device.

2. Sign in with the same email or social login you first used.

3. Your account, your plans, and your holdings are there. Nothing was stored only on the lost phone.

</Steps>

Your account is tied to your sign-in, not to the phone, so a new device sees the same account. There is nothing written on paper to lose and no secret code to type back in.

<Aside type="caution" title="Protect the login you signed in with">
Your account is only as safe as the email or social login you used to sign in. Keep that login secure, turn on its own two-step verification, and do not let anyone else into it. Whoever can sign in as you can approve spends from your account.
</Aside>

## What Monvera never does

Monvera never asks for or shows a private key. There is no seed phrase to write down, and no page on Monvera will ever ask you to enter or reveal a private key. Anyone who does ask for one is not Monvera. See [staying safe from scams](/safety/staying-safe-from-scams/) for how to tell the difference.
