There is no platform fee, no account fee, and no subscription. Monvera covers the network cost of every trade, so investing is free to you. The one cost you pay is the live market spread on each trade, the same small gap between buy and sell price that any market has.

## What you do not pay

You do not pay Monvera to open an account, to hold a plan, or to invest one. There is no monthly charge and no percentage skimmed off your balance. You can invest the "Steady Growth" plan over Apple (AAPL), Nvidia (NVDA), the S&P 500 (SPY), and US Treasuries (SGOV) starting from $1, and none of that dollar goes to a Monvera fee.

Monvera also covers the network cost that every trade on Robinhood Chain carries. You never have to fund a separate balance to pay it, and you never see a charge for it. That is what "free to invest" means on the [invest page](/use/invest-in-one-tap/).

## What you do pay

You pay the live market spread on each trade. When Monvera buys a company for you, it buys at the current asking price, and when you sell, you sell at the current bid. The difference between those two is the spread, and it is set by the market for that company at that moment, not by Monvera. You can check the price you are quoted against the live price on the [explore page](/use/explore-whats-available/) before you commit.

<Aside type="caution" title="The spread is real on small amounts too">
The spread is a percentage of what you trade, so it applies whether you invest $1 or $1,000. On thinly traded companies the spread can be wider. The live quote you see before you tap invest already includes it.
</Aside>

## How Monvera makes money

Monvera earns a small referral from the venue it routes your trades through, paid on the volume it sends there. This does not add a charge on top of your trade. It is a share the venue pays Monvera for bringing the order, and it is why Monvera can run with no platform fee.

Being free to invest does not mean you cannot lose money. You can. See [can I lose money](/safety/can-i-lose-money/). For where Monvera is available, see [where it works](/start/where-its-available/).
