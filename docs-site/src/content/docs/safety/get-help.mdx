If something looks wrong, reach Monvera by email at [support@monvera.best](mailto:support@monvera.best) or on X at [@monvera_best](https://x.com/monvera_best). Those are the two official support channels Monvera runs, and X is the same channel the restricted page points to. Before you worry, a few quick checks explain most surprises.

## Check these first

Most "something is broken" moments have a plain cause. Run these before you reach out.

<Steps>

1. **Your balance dropped.** Check the live prices of your holdings on the [explore page](/use/explore-whats-available/). If the market fell, your plan fell with it. That is prices moving, not a fault. See [can I lose money](/safety/can-i-lose-money/).

2. **The site will not load or shows a restricted page.** Check whether you are connecting from the US, Canada, the UK, or Switzerland, where Monvera is not available. See [where it works](/start/where-its-available/).

3. **An invest did not go through.** A live quote can expire in the seconds before you approve it, and an invest can stop if your account is short of cash. Try again with a fresh quote, or add money first. See [invest in one tap](/use/invest-in-one-tap/).

4. **You cannot find a trade.** Every settled trade is on the public block explorer against your account address, so a trade that completed is there even if the app view is slow to update.

</Steps>

## When to reach out

Email [support@monvera.best](mailto:support@monvera.best) or reach out on X at [@monvera_best](https://x.com/monvera_best) when a check above does not explain what you see, or when you think you found a real fault. Describe what you did, what you expected, and what happened, so the answer comes faster.

<Aside type="caution" title="Only trust the official channels">
Support only comes from [support@monvera.best](mailto:support@monvera.best) and [@monvera_best](https://x.com/monvera_best) on X, and Monvera will never message you first. Anyone who direct-messages you claiming to be Monvera support, or who asks for a private key, is not Monvera. See [staying safe from scams](/safety/staying-safe-from-scams/).
</Aside>

If you lost access to your phone, you do not need support to get back in. You recover the same account by signing in again. See [your account and recovery](/safety/your-account-and-recovery/).
