You may owe tax on your gains, and Monvera does not calculate, withhold, or file anything for you. Selling a holding for more than you paid can be a taxable event where you live, and handling that is yours to do.

## Why there may be tax

There may be tax because you own real companies and funds, and selling them at a gain is usually taxable. If you buy the "Steady Growth" plan over Apple (AAPL), Nvidia (NVDA), the S&P 500 (SPY), and US Treasuries (SGOV) and later sell part of it for more than you paid, that gain can be income the tax authority where you live wants to know about. The rules and rates depend on your country and your own situation.

## What Monvera does not do

Monvera is not a tax service. It does not work out what you owe, it does not take tax out of your trades, and it does not send any form to you or to a tax authority. Monvera is not able to give tax advice, and nothing here is tax advice.

## Keeping your own records

Keep your own record of what you bought and sold, because you can pull the raw history yourself. Every settled trade on your account is on the public block explorer, and Monvera surfaces your [transaction history in the app](/use/withdraw-your-money/). Save the dates, amounts, and prices you buy and sell at, so you or your accountant can work out any tax at year end.

<Aside type="caution" title="Ask a professional for your own case">
Tax rules differ by country and change over time. For what you actually owe, ask a qualified tax professional in your own country rather than relying on a general page.
</Aside>

Tax is separate from whether a plan makes money. You can still lose money on any plan. See [can I lose money](/safety/can-i-lose-money/). For where Monvera is available, see [where it works](/start/where-its-available/).
